- Keep the names in unmodified copies of the project, and in the NOTICE file of modified copies.

- Name your product, service, company or domain "MonkeyITM" or something confusingly similar.
- Call a modified version "MonkeyITM" or "MonkeyITM-VM" (call it something else, and say it is based on MonkeyITM-VM).
- Offer a hosted service under the name, or suggest that the project or its owner endorses, certifies or audits
  your product.
- Use the logos, except in unmodified copies of the project.

## Certification

Only the owner may say that something is "MonkeyITM certified" or "MonkeyITM compatible" in a way that suggests
it has been tested by the project. Truthful statements that you implement the published protocol are fine.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository titled "Trademark request".
